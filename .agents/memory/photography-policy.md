name: Site photography policy
description: Single-use content photos, logo exception, and geographic/license verification.
---

Use each content photograph in only one placement across the whole site, including heroes, cards, sections and fallback backgrounds. Shared branding logos are exempt.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested single-use imagery and confirmed “Keep shared branding logos.”

**How to apply:** Treat responsive sizes and crops as the same underlying photo. Remove unnecessary repeats rather than merely renaming or recropping them. Photo choices may change; the single-placement rule does not.

Verify scenery locations and commercial-use licenses on the individual source page, not from search ranking or a search result's title.

**Why:** Unsplash searches for Meia Praia returned unrelated beaches in Brazil, Florida and elsewhere in Portugal. The user approved another free photo source for Contact when no reliably identified Unsplash image was available.

**How to apply:** Exact-phrase searches and source metadata are more reliable than broad Unsplash queries. Preserve required author, source, license and modification notices on the photo credits page, linked from the footer.

Only source new content photography from Wikimedia Commons under CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, or from Unsplash or Pexels with commercial-use permission. Do not source images from Facebook, news sites, Google Images or other sites. Prefer actual Lagos airfield imagery, relevant microlights and genuine aerial Algarve views.

**Why:** The user explicitly imposed this source restriction and requested aviation-relevant photographs instead of generic stock scenery.

**How to apply:** Check the individual file's license and attribution requirements, including any author website credit. Distinguish illustrative aircraft photographs from Fly365's own fleet, team or historical archive. Keep shared branding logos and existing functional route/location maps separate from the content-photography audit.

Prefer the user's supplied Fly365 photographs for the requested placements. The homepage hero is now the C42-wing sunset over Lagos; the flexwing cockpit-over-orange-cliffs photograph belongs on the Flexwing page, not the homepage.

**Why:** The user supplied Fly365's own photos and explicitly superseded the earlier request to keep the flexwing homepage hero.

**How to apply:** Keep each visible use unique. User-supplied Fly365 photos are approved sources regardless of attachment filename; do not infer an external licence from a filename. Strip EXIF/GPS metadata, correct orientation, crop unwanted bars/watermarks/player icons, and never upscale generated files beyond the source size. The header logo stays.

Keep required photo attribution on a separate `/credits` page with a plain navy hero and a small footer link beside Terms and Privacy. Do not list Unsplash photos, whose licence does not require attribution, or add `/credits` to the sitemap.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a shorter footer and a separate credits page.

**How to apply:** Preserve required Creative Commons credits, licence links, modification notices and any additional author-site attribution when updating the credits data.
