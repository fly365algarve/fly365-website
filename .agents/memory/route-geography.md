---
name: Offshore route geography
description: Distinguishing supplied coastal landmark locations from illustrative flight paths.
---

Keep shore landmark pins at their supplied positions, but place the flight's corresponding turn just offshore when needed to avoid land.

**Why:** The user requires offshore loops; supplied landmark coordinates can sit on a headland rather than over water. Directly connecting coastal landmarks can also cut across intervening promontories.

**How to apply:** Inspect each regenerated static map at full resolution, including the intermediate Luz/Burgau/Ingrina headlands, not just the named endpoints. Preserve the approximate-route warning until actual GPX tracks are supplied.
