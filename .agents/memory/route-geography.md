---
name: Offshore route geography
description: Distinguishing supplied coastal landmark locations from illustrative flight paths.
---

Keep shore landmark pins at their supplied positions, but place coastal flight turns just offshore when needed to avoid headlands. Inland returns are intentional. Keep loops compact and within the user's named itinerary; do not add distant detours to fill the advertised duration.

**Why:** The user replaced wholly offshore loops with varied coastal-and-inland itineraries, then requested tighter routes because their extent was too large for their durations. Coastal landmark coordinates can sit on a headland rather than over water; directly connecting them can cut across intervening promontories.

**How to apply:** Inspect each regenerated static map at full resolution, including the intermediate Luz/Burgau/Ingrina headlands, not just the named endpoints. Check coastal clearance without removing the requested inland visits. Preserve the approximate-route warning until actual GPX tracks are supplied.
