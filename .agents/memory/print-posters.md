---
name: Standalone print poster scope
description: User constraints separating Fly365 printed promotional material from the website.
---

Keep the Fly365 print posters completely separate from the website. Reuse copies of website photos or properly licensed online photos when needed; do not alter website pages, navigation or content to make the posters.

**Why:** The user repeated that the poster work must be completely separate, and explicitly approved existing website images when the original PDF was unavailable.

**How to apply:** Poster-specific promotional copy and contacts in the poster brief are not updates to website business content. Do not propagate them into the website.

Keep poster source artwork and exports outside all public web folders and deployment outputs, under the repository's `design/posters/` directory.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires that the posters cannot be deployed with the website.

**How to apply:** Poster generators and exporters must use the private design directory, never recreate public poster assets or copy them into a website build.

The posters must look like locally printed travel-operator artwork made by a human designer: dominant real photo, condensed headline, confident asymmetric spacing, solid colour, square corners and solid rules. No gradients, fades, glows, shadows, app cards, pill badges, generic filler or AI-generated/composited aircraft.

**Why:** The user stated this was the single most important design constraint.

**How to apply:** Design the print pages from scratch, rather than copying a website or existing poster layout. Use real photos and only supplied claims.
