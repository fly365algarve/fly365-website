---
name: Decorative lines
description: Site-wide restriction on dashed and dotted decoration.
---
Do not introduce decorative dashed or dotted lines in heroes, backgrounds, section dividers, timelines or elsewhere. The only exception is actual routes on the trial-flight map.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested their removal across the whole site, with only this exception.

**How to apply:** Remove decorative line markup and its CSS rather than hiding it. Keep timeline content and numbered steps; they are not themselves dashed or dotted lines.
