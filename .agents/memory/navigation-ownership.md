name: Navigation and booking ownership
description: Keep trial flights and courses distinct, with Prices only a footer summary.
---

Keep trial-flight offers and training courses separate. Detailed training prices belong with courses, not behind a trial/training toggle. Prices is a footer-only summary that preserves the old Prices URL, not another competing booking page.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested simpler navigation and a single home for courses to remove trial/training toggle confusion.

**How to apply:** Informational trial-flight links lead to the trial-flight page. Booking a specific duration carries its trial-flight context into Contact; course enquiries carry training context. Keep generic contact and trip-planning links generic. Preserve existing content and the homepage hero when making navigation changes.
