description: Standing constraint on factual copy and claims in the Fly365 site.

Do not invent facts, stats, claims or testimonials. Short headings and taglines are fine, but factual statements must come from existing site content or facts explicitly supplied by the user.

**Why:** The user repeated this requirement across the redesign briefs.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing factual copy unless the user supplies a correction. Do not fill missing biographies, testimonials, operational claims or statistics with plausible content.

Use Fly365 as the operator name. The user explicitly states “NO COMPANY”; do not add a limited-company identity, company registration number or company-details placeholder to privacy, terms or booking copy.

**Why:** The user clarified the business identity during pre-launch corrections.

**How to apply:** Treat older company wording in source material or templates as superseded. Do not infer incorporation or registration details from the brand name.

Fly365 operates in the Algarve all year round, not only October–April. Its flexwing fleet is P&M Quantum 912 and P&M Quik R; the Deltajet is no longer part of the fleet. Do not compare prices with UK flying schools.

**Why:** Fly365 feedback supplied by the user supersedes older site copy.

**How to apply:** Keep these corrections when reusing older material. User-approved trip copy includes Meia Praia next to the airfield, sociable evenings, and good-value winter accommodation and car hire; do not expand these into unsupported claims.
