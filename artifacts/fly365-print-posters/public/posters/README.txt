FLY365 — STANDALONE A5 PRINT POSTERS

Four pages: fixed-front.html, fixed-back.html, flex-front.html, flex-back.html.
Keep poster.css, fonts/ and img/ beside these HTML files. No network connection
is required to render them. QR codes are inline SVG; fonts are local.
These files are separate from the Fly365 website.

PRINT
148 × 210 mm portrait (A5). Print at actual size / 100%, zero margins,
background colours enabled and browser headers/footers disabled.
For front-and-back PDFs use double-sided printing, flip on the long edge.
The artwork has a 4 mm minimum text safe area, but no additional bleed
outside A5. Ask the shop for an actual-size proof before laminating.
These are browser-produced RGB PDFs, not PDF/X or certified CMYK files.

PHOTO QUALITY
The real G-CISG photo supplied by the existing site is 702 × 468 pixels
(about 120 ppi at full poster width). The cockpit image is 837 × 360
(about 144 ppi at full width). Neither is a 300 ppi print original.
They have not been AI upscaled or composited. Check a printed proof;
replace with larger original photographs if sharper reproduction is needed.
